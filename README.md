# GymOS

A Comprehensive Gym Management Software designed to help gym owners and their teams efficiently run daily operations from a single platform.
</div>

## What GymOS Helps With

- Managing single-gym and multi-branch gym businesses
- Handling member records and lifecycle updates
- Creating and managing subscription packages
- Recording payments, collections, refunds, and coupons
- Managing staff accounts with role-based access controls
- Creating and assigning diet and exercise plans
- Tracking enquiries and follow-ups
- Organizing shifts and streamlining daily operations
- Viewing reports, reviews, and overall business activity
- Sending onboarding and welcome emails for gyms, staff, and members

## Core Features

- Gym onboarding for both single and branch setups
- Centralized admin dashboard
- Member registration and profile management
- Package and plan management
- Payment and coupon tracking
- Staff account creation and access control
- Diet and exercise plan assignment
- Enquiry and follow-up management
- Shift management tools
- Multi-gym support under a unified account
