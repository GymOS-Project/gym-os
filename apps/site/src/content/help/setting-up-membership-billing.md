GymOS is structured to keep billing activity visible from the initial sale through collection tracking and reporting.

## Before you go live

- Finalize the package names, durations, and standard pricing.
- Decide which staff roles can create a sale and which roles can edit billing records.
- Review how discounts and coupon logic should be handled operationally.

## Recommended billing setup order

1. Create package types.
2. Review staff permissions for payment actions.
3. Configure coupon usage rules if your gym uses promotions.
4. Test a member sale and a collection flow end to end.
5. Verify reporting outputs before live usage.

## Operational note

Avoid giving every staff member broad billing access. Cleaner permissions reduce mistakes and make reporting easier to trust later.

## After launch

Check collections daily during the first week. Early review helps identify process gaps before they turn into month-end reconciliation issues.
